I could not have been more stoked to see this 3D printing internship on LinkedIn. Since 2017 when I built my first 3D printer, I have been committed to pioneering and helping my community with additive manufacturing. In response to the pandemic, I started an organization that printed and donated 3000+ face shields to medical and front-line workers. Being able to see the genuinely appreciative faces of recipients was eye opening and inspiring. I realized the amount of impact I can create with technology.

For the past four months, I have been researching and developing a first-ever 3D printer than can print silicone. The challenges that come with extruding a thermosetting polymer as opposed to a thermoplastic can be frustrating to say the least. However, the goal of being able to rapidly design and manufacture pneumatic soft robots and proprietary medical implants motivates me every day.

Alongside this project, I have also been developing our UCSB SAE electric vehicle powertrain as team lead. Although UCSB has competed in SAE Baja in the past, this is our the EV team's first year. Since my team and I have been working on the powertrain from the ground up and without any experience, I have been able to learn so much about how EVs work. Looking back to the beginning of the school year when we learned about how connecting batteries in series versus parallel affects voltage, to now designing our custom battery submodules that include temperature and voltage sensing, is a huge stride in progress that I am extremely proud of. On top of that, being able to compare our submodules with Lucid's submodules that are showcased on Instagram brings a huge grin to my face. 



This 3D printing internship at the most inspiring EV company in the world combines both of my passions and falls directly into my realm of expertise. 

I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications with you in further detail.

Thank you so much for your time and consideration.
%------------------------------------------------
