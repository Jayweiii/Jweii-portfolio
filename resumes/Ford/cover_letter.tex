I am delighted to submit my application for the Mechanical Engineering Intern position at Ford. As a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California - Santa Barbara with extensive hands-on experience in SolidWorks, Designing-for-Manufacturing, CNC Milling, and Formula SAE EV powertrain design,  I am confident that my skills and experiences make me an excellent candidate for this internship.

Currently, I am working as a mechanical engineering intern at Seek Thermal, working closely with our assembly line and suppliers from China on our tolerance stack-ups to improve production throughput. In addition, I design, machine, and 3D print all of our IR sensor testing fixtures, which include working with TECs, solenoids, and heat sinking solutions.

At Gaucho Racing, I lead 60 engineers in the design and manufacturing of our high voltage battery pack, drivetrain, and mounting solutions. At our FSAE Electric competition at Michigan this June, we defied expectations by placing 20th out of 70 in the presentation event as a first-year team. To tackle our design criteria most cost and time effectively, I utilize various manufacturing methods including CNC, welding, 3D printing, laser cutting, soldering, and resin bonding. 

In my role as an undergraduate researcher at Prof. Yangying's Thermofluid Energy Research Laboratory, I recently presented my research on the performance and feasibiilty of using vapor chambers to increase EV battery cooling performance. Additionally, I have worked very closely with a PhD student and manufactured a flow boiling testing apparatus using 3D printing and CNC milling, which went up to the International Space Station in November 2022 as part of a NASA mission. 

I am excited to bring the research, design, and manufacturing skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to Ford. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.
