%------------------------------------------------
As the chief technical director and powertrain lead for Gaucho Racing, the FSAE Electric team at UC Santa Barbara, I have been critical in our efforts to present a tech-passing vehicle at the 2023 Michigan competition. Under my leadership, Gaucho Racing, a team that was formed just one year ago, has finished designing the accumulator, transmission, liquid cooling, chassis, and suspension. Our efforts and capabilities have been acknowledged by industry leaders such as Tesla, Autodesk, and Ansys through sponsorships, and by engineers from Raytheon, Toyon Research, and UCSB through direct engineering collaboration. 

Currently, as we are preparing to move into the manufacturing phase, my main focus is on the integration of our vehicle. I recently had a meeting with Mike O'Neil, the chief design judge of FSAE, to discuss team organization and design targets. Since then, I have rolled out using ClickUp to our 50-member team as a task management software, set up a more effective meeting schedule, and brought on engineers such as Dave Bothman, the previous CNSI manager, and Eric Sandoz, an EE engineer from Toyon Research, to advise us on our designs and manufacturing plans.

In addition to overseeing our engineering progress, the educational and inspirational aspect of FSAE is equally as important to me. One of my primary life objectives is to inspire those around me to pursue their dreams. I created a master doc, which details every component in our vehicle and documents our entire design progress to make it easy for anyone to get started. I host field trips to CNSI workshops, an engineering and research facility, to show our members the resources available to them. I secured a lab space for our liquid cooling test rig, not just for a more accurate characterization of our cooling load, but so that any of our members can partake in a self-designed lab experiment. I have been disappointed by how our professors look down on undergrads and our capabilities and I have struggled against similar problems my entire life against those who are senior to me. Whenever I have tried to take on leadership positions, older adults have always expressed doubt or even gone out of their way to deny my opportunity. Despite those roadblocks, I persevered. I hope to inspire my peers into realizing that nothing is impossible and that no goal is too large.

%------------------------------------------------
