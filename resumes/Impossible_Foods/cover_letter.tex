%------------------------------------------------

Since 2017, when I built my first 3D printer, I have been committed to helping my community with technologies such as additive manufacturing. In response to the pandemic, I started an organization that printed and donated 3000+ face shields to medical and front-line workers. Being able to see the genuinely appreciative faces of recipients was eye opening and inspiring. I realized the amount of impact I can create with technology.

For the past four months, I have been researching and developing a first-ever 3D printer than can print silicone. Being able to additively manufacture silicone, which has properties of heat, water, and UV resistance, flame retardancy, and biocompatibility, will result in huge breakthroughs in countless fields. The challenges that come with extruding a thermosetting polymer as opposed to a thermoplastic can be frustrating to say the least. However, the goal of being able to rapidly design and manufacture pneumatic soft robots and proprietary medical implants motivates me every day. 

Alongside this project, I have also been developing our UCSB SAE electric vehicle powertrain as team lead. Although UCSB has competed in SAE Baja in the past, this is our the EV team's first year. Since my team and I have been working on the powertrain from the ground up and without any experience, creating CAD assemblies using off-the-shelf and custom designed components has been our bread and butter.

Large scale manufacturing is something I have always been extremely interested in but haven't had the opportunity to get my hands dirty with. I have visited the Boeing factory in Seattle and the Haas factory in Oxnard. The scale, efficiency, and organization of such operations impressed me the moment I stepped on the catwalk. 

With my multidisciplinary experiences in CAD, 3D printing, Arduino \& PWM motor control, workshop machining, and working with and managing project teams, I believe I can meaningufully contribute to Impossible Food's mission of saving the planet as deliciously as possible. 

I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications with you in further detail.

Thank you so much for your time and consideration.
%------------------------------------------------
