As the Technical Director and Powertrain Lead for Gaucho Racing, the FSAE Electric team at UC Santa Barbara, I am delighted to submit my application for the Mechanical Test Engineering Intern position at BAE Systems. With my extensive experience in 3D printing, CAD, CNC machining, and high voltage systems design, I am confident in my ability to support BAE Systems in product development, integration and testing.

At Gaucho Racing, I lead the design and manufacturing of our safe, modular, and repairable high voltage battery pack, drivetrain, and mounting solutions. This includes establishing and meeting our performance and safety targets using Ansys FEA, Matlab Simulink, and physical testing. In order to meet our tight deadlines, I have become very skilled in various manufacturing methods including , 3D printing, CNC, welding, laser cutting, soldering, and resin bonding. Using these processes, I have created carbon fiber battery mounting brackets, topology optimized steel differential manifolds, and fiberglass wet layup composites. My current ambitions are to combine 3D printed geometries, such as gyroids, with phase-change thermofluidics, to increase the efficiency of heat exchangers. This will allow for the improved performance and increased operational temperature range for a variety of power electronics.

In addition, I have thorough machine shop and fabrication experience due to my role as a Workshop Wizard and 3D Printer Tool Lead at the California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI). I have managed, repaired, and improved processes for our Haas Super Mini Mill and all types of our 3D printers, including FDM, SLA, DLP, and PolyJet. I spend about 10 hours per week in the shop designing and manufacturing researchers' experiment setups. As a result, I have developed a deep understanding of the different design and material considerations of additive manufacturing, subtractive manufacturing (CNC \& manual machining), chemical post-processes (such as acetone vapor smoothing), and even combining these different manufacturing methods in creating a single part.

In conclusion, I am excited to bring the skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to BAE Systems and ensure that equipment meets design requirements. Thank you for considering my application, and I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.
