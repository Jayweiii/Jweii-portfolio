I am delighted to submit my application for the Engineering Intern position at Joby Aviation. As a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California - Santa Barbara with extensive hands-on experience in Formula SAE EV powertrain design, electrochemical cell modelling, SolidWorks, Ansys, Designing-for-Manufacturing, and MATLAB/Simulink circuit modelling, I am confident that my multidisciplinary skill-set and experiences make me an excellent candidate for this internship.

For the past summer, I worked as a mechanical engineering intern at Seek Thermal, designing and machining all of our IR sensor testing fixtures, which include working with TECs, solenoids, and heat sinking solutions. Additionally, I directly supported our assembly line by communicating with injection molding suppliers from China on our tolerance stack-ups and improving engineering drawings to improve production throughput. 

At Gaucho Racing, I have been leading 60 engineers in the design and manufacturing of our wire bonded battery pack, drivetrain, and mounting solutions. For our second ever EV that we are building, I have coupled the vehicle's entire mechanical-electrical-thermal system in Simulink as it runs through a 22km endurance race track. I am now working closely with Hesse Mechatronics in the design and manufacturing of our new battery pack, utilizing their ultrasonic wire bonder for fusible links.  

In my role as an undergraduate researcher at Prof. Yangying's Thermofluid Energy Research Laboratory, I recently presented my research on the performance and feasibility of using vapor chambers to increase EV battery cooling performance. Additionally, I have worked very closely with a PhD student and manufactured a flow boiling testing apparatus using 3D printing and CNC milling, which went up to the International Space Station in November 2022 as part of a NASA mission. 

I am excited to bring the research, design, and manufacturing skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to Joby Aviation. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.
