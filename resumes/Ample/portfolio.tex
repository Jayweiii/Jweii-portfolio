I have always been intrigued by the concept of creativity. Where do new ideas come from? As I gained more problem solving experience through my pursuit of engineering projects, whether success or failure, I realized that new ideas aren't always "new." Ideas, and many parts of our identity, are often the synthesis of previous experiences; components of an idea can be traced back to the past. By keeping this concept in mind, I have been able to tackle challenges much more fearlessly, because both success and failure can be beneficial outcomes.

With this fearlessness in mind, I am constantly envisioning ways that technology can improve my surrounding community. One of the ways that I have accomplished this is through 3D printing. When lockdown started, I started an organization that printed and donated 3000+ face shields to medical and front-line workers. To provide materials for the dozens of volunteers that were willing to buy printers and help produce face shields, I launched a GoFundMe campaign that raised over three thousand dollars in funds. Since most volunteers did not have 3D printing experience, I created a step-by-step video series that guided them through the process of setting up their printers and producing quality prints. Within a week, I was churning out and donating 100 face shields per week with the help of my volunteers. Being able to see the genuinely appreciative faces of recipients was eye opening and inspiring. I realized the amount of impact I can create with technology. 

My career dream is to make flying cars a reality. As a little kid, I loved gawking at every car on the road and memorizing their makes, models, and even license plates. Being able to soar across the sky on a quick and sustainable vehicle that can travel anywhere would be so magical. To achieve my fantasy, I aim to invest myself in engineering projects that will help me grow as a person and improve my skillset.
\end{cvletter}
