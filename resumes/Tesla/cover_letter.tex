As the technical director and powertrain lead for Gaucho Racing, the FSAE Electric team at UC Santa Barbara, I am delighted to submit this engineering intern application. I have been critical in our efforts to presenting a tech-passing vehicle at the 2023 Michigan competition and I hope to demonstrate my experiences in power electronics, mechanics, mechanics, and thermofluidics at Tesla. 

Under my leadership at Gaucho Racing, we have finished designing the accumulator, precharge/discharge circuit, transmission, liquid cooling, chassis, and suspension in just one year. Our efforts and capabilities have been acknowledged by our sponsors, which include Tesla, Autodesk, and Ansys, and by professional engineers from Raytheon, Toyon Research, and UCSB, of whom we collaborate with through design reviews, discussions, and meetings. 

Currently, as we are preparing to move into the manufacturing phase, my main focus is on the integration of our vehicle. I recently had a meeting with Mike O'Neil, the chief design judge of FSAE, to discuss team organization and design targets. Since then, I have rolled out using ClickUp to our 50-member team as a task management software, set up a more effective meeting schedule, and brought on engineers such as Dave Bothman, the previous CNSI manager, and Eric Sandoz, an EE engineer from Toyon Research, to advise us on our designs and manufacturing plans.

One of my missions in my engineering journey, which can be seen in our collaborative and efficient FSAE team, is to always inspire and educate my peers. Some of my efforts to make hands-on engineering experiences and resources more accessible include creating detailed and organized documentation, hosting tours to the California NanoSystems Institute, and securing a lab space for our liquid cooling test rig. I hope to inspire my peers in believing that nothing is impossible and no goal is too large.

I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications with you in further detail. Interning at Tesla would be an exciting opportunity for me to experience the industry leading technology, collaboration, and inter-team integration that is behind Tesla's success. 

Thank you so much for your time and consideration.
