As the technical director and powertrain lead for Gaucho Racing, the FSAE Electric team at UC Santa Barbara, I am delighted to submit this engineering intern application. 

I have been working closely with Eric Sandoz, Toyon Research's Deputy Director of the Antenna and RF Systems team, on our tractive system circuitry for the past few months. This includes an overall system diagram and a precharge circuit that activates during the start-up sequence of our vehicle to limit inrush current over the motor controller. Through our bi-weekly meetings, Eric has been a massive help, teaching us schematic design practices and how to communicate design decisions clearly.

In addition to what I have learned about power electronics, I have extensive experience with 3D printing, CNC machining, and thermofluidics. My role as Workshop Wizard at the California NanoSystems Institute allows me to work directly with many research groups on campus and to explore my curiosity in many engineering disciplines. 

I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications with you in further detail. Interning at Toyon Research would be an exciting opportunity for me to experience the industry leading technology, collaboration, and inter-team integration in a professional environment.
