I am delighted to submit my application for the Mechanical Engineering Intern position at NREL. As a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California - Santa Barbara, my extensive background in 3D printing, CAD, CNC machining, thermofluidics, and high voltage systems makes me a strong candidate to contribute to your team's purposeful innovation.

At Gaucho Racing, I lead the design and manufacturing of our safe, modular, and repairable high voltage battery pack, drivetrain, and mounting solutions. To tackle our design criteria most cost and time effectively, I utilize various manufacturing methods including CNC, welding, 3D printing, laser cutting, soldering, and resin bonding. 

In my role as an undergraduate researcher at the UCSB Thermofluid Energy Research Laboratory, I am combining 3D printed geometries, such as gyroids, with phase-change thermofluidics, to increase the efficiency of thermal management systems. This will allow for the improved performance and increased operational temperature range for a variety of power electronics and batteries.

Additionally, I have thorough machine shop and fabrication experience from my role as a Workshop Wizard and 3D Printer Tool Lead at the California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI). I manage, repair, and improve processes for our Haas Super Mini Mill and all types of our 3D printers, including FDM, SLA, DLP, and PolyJet. My hands-on experiences in manufacturing researchers' experimental setups has honed my understanding of the different design and material considerations of additive manufacturing, subtractive manufacturing (CNC \& manual machining), chemical post-processes (such as acetone vapor smoothing), and even combining these different manufacturing methods in creating a single part.

I am excited to bring the skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to NREL. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.
