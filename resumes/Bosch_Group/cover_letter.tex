I am delighted to submit my application for the Mechanical/Automation Engineering Intern position at Bosch Group. As a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California - Santa Barbara with extensive hands-on experience in test rig manufacturing, SolidWorks, and Formula SAE EV powertrain design, I believe I have the skills and experience necessary to excel in this role.

As a Workshop Wizard and 3D Printer Tool Lead at the CNSI Microfluidics lab and Innovation Workshop, I manufacture and help design campus researchers' experimental setups. Additioanlly, I manage, repair, and improve processes for our Haas Super Mini Mill and all types of our 3D printers, including FDM, SLA, DLP, and PolyJet. These experiences have honed my understanding of the different design and material considerations of additive manufacturing, subtractive manufacturing (CNC \& manual machining), chemical post-processes (such as acetone vapor smoothing), and even combining these different manufacturing methods in creating a single part.

In my role as an undergraduate researcher at Prof. Yangying's Thermofluid Energy Research Laboratory, I have been contributing to their research of analyzing flow instabilities due to micro-structures. I have worked very closely with a PhD student and manufactured the entire flow boiling testing apparatus using 3D printing and CNC milling. Additionally, as a personal research project under the mentorship of Yangying, I have been exploring combining 3D printed internal geometries, such as gyroids, with gas-liquid double phase change, to increase the efficiency of heat exchangers. 

I am excited to bring the research, testing, and manufacturing skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to Bosch Group. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.
