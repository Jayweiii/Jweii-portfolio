%------------------------------------------------

I am delighted to submit my application for the Mechanical Engineering Intern position at Sensata Technologies. With my extensive experience in 3D printing, CAD, CNC machining, and high voltage systems design, I am confident in my ability to improve Sensata's production line.

As the Technical Director and Powertrain Lead at Gaucho Racing, UCSB's Formula SAE Electric program, I have designed our high voltage battery pack, drivetrain, and liquid cooling solutions. I utilize calculations and simulations done in Matlab and Ansys to support my design decisions. To take advantage of my expertise in 3D printing, I have transitioned many of our mounts to carbon fiber filled nylon to reduce weight and cost while maximizing structural integrity. My current ambitions are to combine 3D printed geometries, such as gyroids, with phase-change thermofluidics, to increase the efficiency of heat exchangers. This will allow for the improved performance and increased operational temperature range for a variety of power electronics.

As a Workshop Wizard and 3D Printer Tool Lead at the California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI), I have managed, repaired, and improved processes for our Haas Super Mini Mill and all types of our 3D printers, including FDM, SLA, DLP, and PolyJet. I support researchers throughout the entire design and manufacturing process to ensure their parts meet design specifications at the lowest cost. As a result, I have developed a thorough understanding of the different design and material considerations of additive manufacturing, subtractive manufacturing (CNC \& manual machining), and chemical post-processes (such as acetone vapor smoothing). Additionally, I have been able to combine the use of these different manufacturing methods in creating a single part.

In conclusion, I am excited to bring the skills that I have accrued through hands-on engineering experiences to Sensata and improve various aspects of Sensata's production. Thank you for considering my application, and I look forward to discussing this internship opportunity and my qualifications in further detail.

%------------------------------------------------
